What to check before ordering

Measure for fit

Use the size chart for the selected variant. For a hoodie or tee, compare chest width and body length with a garment you own. For shoes, match your foot length to the seller’s chart.

Review QC photos

If warehouse quality-control photos are available, match them to your chosen color and size. Look for sole wear on shoes, seam alignment on clothing and zipper or strap attachment on bags.

Estimate delivery cost

Add any local delivery, agent fees, international shipping and applicable taxes to the item price. Check whether the route uses actual or volumetric weight, especially for boxed shoes and bulky jackets.

HooBuy spreadsheet FAQ

Where do the product links go?

Each product name opens its HipobuyFind listing. Both buttons at the top open the HipobuyFind homepage.

Can I use a listing with HooBuy?

Open the HipobuyFind listing and look for the original seller URL. If you find one, check whether HooBuy accepts that source and can order your selected variant before paying.

Are seller photos the same as QC photos?

Seller photos illustrate the listing. Warehouse QC photos, when available, show the unit received for inspection. Use those photos to confirm the color, label and visible condition of your item.

Does the listed price include international shipping?

The directory shows reference item prices only. Use your destination, estimated parcel dimensions and shipping route to calculate the delivered cost.
